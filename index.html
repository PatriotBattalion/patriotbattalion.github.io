About the Program

The Patriot Battalion Army JROTC program develops citizenship, leadership, and self-discipline in cadets through classroom instruction, drill, physical training, and community service. Cadets progress through a structured rank system while competing on the Battalion's Raider, Drill, Color Guard, and Air Rifle teams.

Beyond competition, the Battalion represents the school at parades, ceremonies, and community events throughout the year, guided by its instructors and cadet chain of command.
