What is JCLC?

The JROTC Cadet Leadership Camp (JCLC) is a summer leadership camp where cadets from across the region train together in leadership, teamwork, and outdoor skills — including rappelling, orienteering, team-building obstacle courses, and small-unit leadership exercises. It's one of the most demanding and rewarding experiences a cadet can have in the program, and it's open by application to cadets recommended by the instructor staff.

⭐ Cadets who attend JCLC have a higher chance of advancing in rank and becoming an officer.
