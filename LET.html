The Battalion / LET Levels

LET Levels

Leadership Education and Training — the Army JROTC curriculum, one level for each year a cadet is in the program.

8th Grade

JLC — Junior Leadership Corps

Unit 1 Cadet Notebook — same book as LET 1

  • The first step into the Battalion for 8th graders
  • Customs and courtesies, uniform wear, and basic drill
  • Teamwork, goal setting, and personal responsibility
  • Prepares cadets to move up to LET 1 in 9th grade
9th Grade

LET 1 — The Emerging Leader

Unit 1 Cadet Notebook

  • JROTC history, purpose, customs and courtesies
  • Uniform standards and drill basics
  • Study skills, goal setting, and decision-making
  • Handling stress, anger, and conflict
  • Cadet Challenge fitness and service learning
10th Grade

LET 2 — The Developing Leader

Unit 2 Cadet Notebook

  • Leadership styles, principles, and ethics
  • Communication and writing skills
  • Leading a squad in drill and building teamwork
  • First aid, health, and nutrition
  • Citizenship and how American democracy works
11th Grade

LET 3 — The Supervising Leader

Unit 3 Cadet Notebook

  • Battalion staff roles, running meetings, and planning projects
  • Supervising others and managing your own time and goals
  • Ethical decision-making and negotiation
  • College and career planning, including a personal portfolio
  • Being a responsible citizen in the community
12th Grade

LET 4 — The Managing Leader

Unit 4 textbook

  • Managing the Battalion as a senior cadet leader
  • Teaching, mentoring, and giving feedback
  • Motivating teams and running company and battalion drill
  • Leading a service-learning project
  • Citizen rights and responsibilities
Electives

Unit 5 — Continuing Education for Leaders

Unit 5 textbook, used across all levels

  • Extra chapters instructors add throughout the program
  • Topics like first aid, map reading and land navigation
  • Personal finance, communication, and American history
