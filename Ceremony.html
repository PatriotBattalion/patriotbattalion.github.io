Promotions

Cadets advance through the Battalion's rank structure by instructor decision, performance, and a formal promotion board. There are two tracks — the NCO (enlisted) track, which every cadet starts on, and the Officer track for cadets selected for command and staff positions.

NCO Track
  • C/PVTCadet Private
  • C/PFCCadet Private First Class
  • C/CPLCadet Corporal
  • C/SGTCadet Sergeant
  • C/SSGCadet Staff Sergeant
  • C/SFCCadet Sergeant First Class
  • C/MSGCadet Master Sergeant
  • C/1SGCadet First Sergeant
  • C/SGMCadet Sergeant Major
  • C/CSMCadet Command Sergeant Major
Officer Track
  • C/2LTCadet Second Lieutenant
  • C/1LTCadet First Lieutenant
  • C/CPTCadet Captain
  • C/MAJCadet Major
  • C/LTCCadet Lieutenant Colonel

Promotion Board

Step 1 — Eligibility

Eligibility for the promotion board is decided by the instructors.

Step 2 — Board Prep

Candidates review rank structure, uniform standards, and battalion history.

Step 3 — The Board

Cadets appear before a panel of senior cadets and staff for a formal Q&A and inspection.

Step 4 — Pinning

New rank is awarded at the next battalion formation or ceremony.

ℹ️ 
Grade note: the promotion board caps rank by grade level — for example, an 8th grader tops out at C/SGT (E-5) until they move up a grade.
